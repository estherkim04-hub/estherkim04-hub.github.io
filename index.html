About

Building intelligent systems that understand people and context.

I am interested in AI systems that connect perception, sensing, and real-world interaction. My recent work focuses on electrooculography (EOG)-based gaze understanding, where I study participant-independent viewing-distance and gaze-direction classification using machine-learning and deep-learning models.

I am especially interested in wearable and situated AI, multimodal context modelling, adaptive interfaces, and robust visual recognition.

Projects

Selected research projects

IXLab · 2026–Present

EOG-Based Gaze Direction & Viewing-Distance Classification

Developing ML/DL pipelines for six-channel EOG data from 20 participants. Compared classical models with CNN and Transformer architectures and evaluated participant-independent generalization using leave-one-subject-out cross-validation.

WBridge Research Program · 2026

Thermal UAV Missing-Person Detection under Unseen Domains

Co-developed a YOLOv5-based single-domain generalization framework using Fourier Domain Adaptation, thermal contrast augmentation, and stabilization losses for cross-dataset search-and-rescue detection.

Medical AI · 2026

Clinically Reliable Synthetic Chest X-Ray Dataset Construction

Built a multi-agent validation workflow for synthetic MIMIC-CXR samples and used validated augmentation to improve pneumothorax classification performance.
